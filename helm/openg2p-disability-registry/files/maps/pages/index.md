<!--
  Rules this page depends on:

  1. NOTHING may be called `name` — not a SQL alias, not a GeoJSON property.
     Evidence backs each map input with a callable proxy, and a `name` field
     collides with the read-only Function.name, killing the map with
     "Cannot assign to read only property 'name'". Everything uses `area_name`.

  2. Drill is driven by CLICKING an area (the `name=` prop publishes the clicked
     row into inputs). That is the whole reason this surface is Evidence and not
     another Superset dashboard.

  3. Nothing here names a level. Sources are level1/2/3_summary (the reporting
     views unpack geography positionally) and boundaries are level1/2/3.geojson.
     Headings are generated from the installed pack by sync_pack.py.

  WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  The map answers one question at every level: where are the people whose
  support needs are not being met? Colour is always UNMET NEED, never population
  — a map of registrant density just reproduces a map of where people live and
  tells an administrator nothing they can act on.

  The second map breaks that need down by DOMAIN, because "unmet need" alone
  does not say who should act. Assistive technology is a procurement problem,
  housing support a public-works problem, human assistance a caregiver-allowance
  problem. Those go to different budgets and different ministries.
-->

```sql national
select
  sum(registrants)            as registrants,
  sum(unmet_need)             as unmet_need,
  sum(unmet_and_unenrolled)   as unmet_and_unenrolled,
  sum(enrolled)               as enrolled,
  sum(severe_or_profound)     as severe_or_profound,
  sum(children)               as children,
  sum(certificate_expired)    as certificate_expired,
  sum(reassessment_overdue)   as reassessment_overdue
from registry.level1_summary
```

### What kind of support is missing here?

```sql by_domain
select support_domain, sum(unmet) as unmet, sum(people_with_unmet_need) as people
from registry.level2_unmet_by_domain
where '${inputs.selected_level1.pcode}' = '' or parent_pcode = '${inputs.selected_level1.pcode}'
group by support_domain
order by unmet desc
```

<BarChart
    data={by_domain}
    x=support_domain
    y=unmet
    title="Unmet support records by domain"
    swapXY=true
/>

```sql top_items
select area_name, support_domain, top_unmet_type, unmet, people_with_unmet_need
from registry.level2_unmet_by_domain
where unmet > 0
  and ('${inputs.selected_level1.pcode}' = '' or parent_pcode = '${inputs.selected_level1.pcode}')
order by unmet desc
limit 25
```

<!--
  `top_unmet_type` is the single most-requested item in that area and domain —
  the line an administrator can take to a procurement plan.
-->

  <Column id=unmet title="Unmet records" fmt='#,##0' contentType=colorscale />

</DataTable>
